Privacy Policy

Application: yt_sisyphus · Last updated: 8 June 2026

yt_sisyphus ("the App") is a personal, single-user tool operated by Paweł Halladin ("the Operator") that publishes the Operator's own educational videos to the Operator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data the App accesses

To publish videos, the App uses the TikTok Content Posting API. With the Operator's OAuth authorization, the App receives and stores:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the Operator's own TikTok account;
  • basic account identifiers returned by TikTok that are required to complete an upload.

The App publishes video files and their captions that the Operator has created. It does not read, collect, or process data belonging to any other TikTok users.

2. How data is stored

All credentials are stored locally on the Operator's own computer, in a local configuration file. They are never transmitted to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints, and are never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

3. How data is used

Tokens are used exclusively to authenticate requests to the TikTok API for the purpose of uploading the Operator's videos. The App performs no analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling, and contains no third-party trackers.

4. Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The only external service the App communicates with is TikTok itself, as required to perform uploads.

5. Data retention and deletion

Tokens persist locally only until they expire or are deleted. The Operator can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok account settings and may delete the local token file, which removes all stored credentials.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a private tool not directed to children and does not knowingly process any children's data.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
